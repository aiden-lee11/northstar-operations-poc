Devin and Cursor overlap heavily — prove it with real work05 / 05

Run a one-month
Devin pilot.

There is a lot of overlap between the two tools, and we believe Devin does everything Cursor does for your team and more. A month of real work is how you confirm that — give a group of engineers Devin, keep the rest on Cursor, and build one small internal tool along the way.

Is the work good?What ships, what gets thrown out

What does it cost?Licenses, usage, upkeep
